Siskin

Your car. Your music.

Siskin plays your Plex music library on your car's built-in screen. It runs on Android Automotive OS, the version of Android that runs the infotainment system in cars from Volvo, Polestar, GM and others.

It isn't a phone app mirrored onto the dashboard. Siskin installs on the car itself, shows up next to the car's other audio sources, and works with the car's own screen and controls. Your phone can stay in your pocket.

What it does

  • Your library Browse your playlists, artists and albums, plus the Discover rows your Plex server builds. You choose which tabs the car shows.
  • Mixes and radio Artist, Playlist and Decade Mixes give you a shuffled queue you can see. Artist Radio keeps playing similar music when the artist's own songs run out.
  • Decades Browse your library by decade. Each decade gets a mosaic of its album covers.
  • Picks up where you left off Your queue is still there the next time you start the car.

What it doesn't do

There's no phone app, no Siskin account, and no Siskin server. The app talks to Plex and nothing else.

The developer gets nothing. No analytics, no crash reports, no telemetry. The privacy policy has the details.

Get Siskin

Siskin is on Google Play for cars with Google built in. Open the Play Store on your car's screen and search for Siskin, or use the link below. You'll need a Plex account and a Plex server with a music library. Everyone in a Plex Home can listen as themselves, managed users included.
