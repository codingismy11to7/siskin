Siskin plays the music on your own Plex server on your car's built-in screen.

It runs on the car's head unit, not on your phone. Once you're signed in, there's nothing to plug in or pair. Siskin shows up next to the car's other audio sources and works with the car's own screen and controls.

WHAT IT DOES

• Browse your playlists, artists and albums, plus the Discover rows your Plex server builds
• Choose which tabs the car shows, and in what order
• Artist, Playlist and Decade Mixes give you a shuffled queue you can see
• Artist Radio keeps playing similar music when the artist's own songs run out (turn it on in Settings)
• Browse your library by decade
• Search your library
• Your queue is still there the next time you start the car
• Plays your files as they are, with no transcoding, including lossless FLAC
• Everyone in a Plex Home can listen as themselves, managed users included
• Sign in by scanning a QR code or entering a short code, so there's no typing in the car
• Reports what you play to Plex, so your listening history and resume positions stay correct

WHAT YOU NEED

A Plex account and a Plex Media Server with a music library. Siskin doesn't host or provide any music. It plays music you already have.

PRIVACY

There are no ads, no analytics, and no account with anyone but Plex. Siskin sends nothing to its developer, and the only servers it talks to are Plex's and your own.
