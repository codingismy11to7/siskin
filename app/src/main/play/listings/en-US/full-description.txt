Siskin plays the music from your own Plex server on your car's built-in screen.

It runs on the head unit itself. This is not a phone app being projected into the car — there is no phone involved once you are signed in, and nothing to plug in or pair. Siskin appears alongside the car's other media sources, and behaves like one.

WHAT IT DOES

• Browse your library by playlist, artist or album
• Search across your library
• Plays your files as they are, with no transcoding — including lossless FLAC
• Sign in by scanning a QR code or entering a short code, so there is no typing while you are in the car
• Reports playback back to Plex, so your listening history and resume positions stay correct

WHAT YOU NEED

A Plex account and a Plex Media Server with a music library. Siskin does not host or provide any music — it plays what is already yours.

WHAT IT DOES NOT DO

No ads. No tracking. No analytics. No account with anyone but Plex. Siskin sends nothing to its developer, and the only servers it talks to are Plex's and your own.

Siskin is deliberately small. It plays your music in your car, and that is all it tries to be.

Siskin is an unofficial client. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Plex Inc. "Plex" is used only to describe the service that Siskin connects to.
