• The three shuffle rows are now Artist Mix, Playlist Mix and Decade Mix. Each hands over a queue that is already shuffled, so the queue you see is the queue that plays.
• Tapping a Mix no longer flips the car's shuffle control, and tapping a track no longer switches it off. Only the car's own shuffle button changes shuffle mode.
• "Use the car's shuffle" is gone from Settings — a Mix is now always a shuffled queue.
