• The More tab has Discover: the hub rows your Plex server builds from what you listen to. Each opens onto its full contents and can be mixed, and each wears a mosaic of its own covers.
• Settings has a Customize tabs screen — drag the browse destinations into the order you want, and the top three become the car's tabs.
• Tapping the version in Settings opens a debug screen: the addresses known for your server, which one is in use, and a way back to the server picker.
